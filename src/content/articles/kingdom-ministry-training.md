Bridging the Theology and Practice of Spiritual Gifts Through Hands-On Training.

## Jesus proclaimed and demonstrated the gospel of the Kingdom.

Jesus not only shared the good news, He also demonstrated it in the power of the Holy Spirit (Matthew 4:23). We are called to follow in His model, but how do you get started in doing what Jesus did? Get equipped through one of the options below.

### 1. Hearing God For Self

*Teaching, Tools, and Activations*

In this first session, we will unpack and practice hearing God for self. Learn hands-on activations in ways God speaks and how you can hear Him practically.

[View training slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/13xAfMBJuACeUwJ0RqmWCbCdllR6SYCM1MTHsE1aEYnM/edit?usp=sharing)

[Watch the training video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHq6WdQoNDU)

### 2. Practicing Prophecy

*Teaching, Tools, and Activations*

In this second session, we look at prophecy in the New Testament to understand the why, who, and how of prophecy 101. Learn hands-on activations in growing in giving and receiving prophetic words.

[View training slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/13r7HYZ_egjPmIMVMCxx6aV-6NNiTl2Tn9sCyRRq6Y2g/edit?usp=sharing)

[Watch the training video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qkqjphLSXRk)

### 3. Building a Community of Practice

*Teaching, Tools, and Activations*

In this module, we will experience and learn to create a community of practice that integrates Kingdom Ministry Skills into a local church network context.

[View training slides](https://bit.ly/1COR14)

[![Screenshot of a 1 Corinthians 14 meeting](/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Screenshot-2025-01-22-at-9.10.54%E2%80%AFAM.webp)](https://rumble.com/v31o6gg-1cor14-meeting-how-to-lead-and-start-one.html)

### 4. Simple 5 Step Healing Prayer Model (GAPAP)

*Teaching, Tools, and Activations*

Jesus both models & commands us to pray for the sick. This practical model, adapted from the 5 Step Model by John Wimber, trains any believer to learn to pray more effectively for physical healing.

[View training slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1-qPkBIoF4OZ-EglqCi1FBiuzKkjB9eTIWKD3URhLZ_Y/edit?usp=sharing)

[Watch the training video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FmjPyUM-1SA)

### 5. Power Evangelism (How It Works)

*Teaching, Tools, and Activations*

Demonstrating the Kingdom of God was a central part of Jesus's ministry as He went about Galilee. We can learn to be equipped to practically walk in demonstrating the kingdom through prophecy, healing, and deliverance. In this training, we review what Power Evangelism includes in terms of hearing God, prophecy, and healing prayer, and practice simple skills for engaging.

[View training slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1ROW4QDrGrWMjGYzqPK8pFft51B7Bla9SYyHfs5q6X9g/edit?usp=sharing)

[Watch the training video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z_DIWdOHDxg)

## Building a Healthy Kingdom Ministry Learning Community

We have found that as networks begin to implement Spirit-led Kingdom Ministry, most have questions around building in guardrails for healthy practice.

As a practicing network in New York City, we would like to offer a resource below as an emerging model for healthy practice. The discipleship packet represents an integrated model of one way to implement building a community of practice into short and mid term discipleship.

The structure models a regular equipping and self-correcting rhythm for emerging and established networks.
