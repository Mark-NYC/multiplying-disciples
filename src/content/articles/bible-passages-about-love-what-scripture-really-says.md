**Love** is the through-line of Scripture. **Bible passages about love** from Genesis to Revelation reveals God as love and calls His people to reflect it. If you're searching for **biblical verses about love**, you're asking the right question—because **love in the Bible** isn't just a feeling. It's the core of God's character and the heartbeat of discipleship.

## What the Bible Actually Means by "Love"

**Biblical teachings on love** start with God. Love in Scripture is *agape*—self-giving, covenant-keeping, sacrificial. It's not emotion-driven or conditional. God's love isn't triggered by your performance; it flows from His nature. This moved Him to create, to covenant with Israel, and ultimately to send His Son. When the Bible talks about it, it's talking about God's relentless commitment to His people and His call for us to do the same—with loyalty, action, and sacrifice.

## Key **Bible Passages** About God's Love

**1 John 4:8** – "God is love." This isn't metaphor. It's identity. God doesn't just act lovingly; He *is* love. Every action flows from this core reality.

**John 3:16** – "For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son." The ultimate **love quote from the Bible**. God didn't send a prophet or a program—He gave His Son. Love costs.

**John 14:23** – Jesus replied, "Anyone who **loves me will obey my teaching.** My Father will love them, and we will come to them and make our home with them." Learn [three key obedience habits of disciples worth multiplying](/the-3-core-habits-of-a-disciple/).

**Romans 5:8** – "But God demonstrates his own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us." God's love doesn't wait for you to clean up. It meets you in the mess.

**Ephesians 2:4-5** – "But because of his great love for us, God, who is rich in mercy, made us alive with Christ." You were dead. His love brought you back. That's the gospel.

## **Bible Passages** About Loving Others

**John 13:34-35** – "A new command I give you: Love one another." Jesus makes this the family badge. You're known by how you [treat each other as His kingdom of priests](/royal-priest-strategy-explodes-disciple-making-movement-worldwide/), not your theology alone.

**1 Corinthians 13:4-7** – The famous passage. Love is patient, kind, not envious or boastful. This isn't romantic fluff—it's a blueprint for discipleship and community.

**Matthew 22:37-39** – "Love the Lord your God... Love your neighbor as yourself." Jesus compresses the entire Law into two commands. **Scripture on love and relationships** doesn't get clearer than this.

**Romans 12:9-10** – "Love must be sincere. Hate what is evil; cling to what is good." Real love has backbone. It's not tolerance—it's truth wrapped in **compassion**.

## **Bible Passages** About Love in Action

**Luke 10:25-37** – The Good Samaritan. Love crosses enemy lines, interrupts your schedule, and costs you something. It's not theoretical.

**Galatians 5:13-14** – "Serve one another humbly in love. For the entire law is fulfilled in keeping this one command: 'Love your neighbor as yourself.'" Faith without action is dead. **Love and compassion in the Bible** always move.

**1 John 3:18** – "Let us not love with words or speech but with actions and in truth." Stop talking. Start doing.

**Matthew 25:35-40** – "Whatever you did for one of the least of these... you did for me." Jesus identifies with the hungry, the stranger, the prisoner. Your care for them is care for Him.

## **Bible Passages** About Love - God Transforming Us

**Romans 8:38-39** – Nothing can separate you from God's love. Not failure, not demons, not death. This isn't motivational—it's anchor-deep truth for when life falls apart.

**Ephesians 3:17-19** – Paul prays you'd grasp "how wide and long and high and deep is the love of Christ." God's love isn't something you master—it's something that roots you, grounds you, redefines you.

**1 John 4:19** – "We love because he first loved us." You don't generate it. You receive it, then overflow it. God's love is the source; yours is the echo.

## Short **Bible Passages About Love**

- **John 15:13** – "Greater love has no one than this: to lay down one's life."
- **Proverbs 10:12** – "Hatred stirs up conflict, but love covers over all wrongs."
- **Colossians 3:14** – "And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together."
- **1 Peter 4:8** – "Above all, love each other deeply."
- **Song of Solomon 8:7** – "Many waters cannot quench love."
- **Psalm 136:26** – "Give thanks to the God of heaven. His love endures forever."

## How These Passages Fit Together

God is love. He demonstrated it by sending Jesus. Now He commands His people to love Him fully and others sacrificially. That shows up in action—feeding, serving, forgiving, laying down your life. And as you experience His love, it transforms you from the inside out. It's not compartmentalized—it's total.

## Final Encouragement

Pick one passage from this list. Read it slowly. Ask God to make it real in your life today. Then find one person to serve the way Christ served you. Don't overthink it. Just move.
