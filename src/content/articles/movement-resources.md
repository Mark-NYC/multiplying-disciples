Once fruit of multiplying disciples begins to emerge, new tools are needed to learn to steward the emerging network. These tools may be unhelpful to share too soon before disciples have the fruit in which to obey and put them into practice. It is helpful to cast vision for multiplying churches and leaders using the resources below from fruitful movement networks such as [#NoPlaceLeft](https://noplaceleft.net/), [1BodyChurch](http://1body.church/), or [BigLife](https://big.life/).
