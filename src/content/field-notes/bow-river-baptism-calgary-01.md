After placing her faith in Christ, a young woman was baptized in the Bow
River at Bowness in Calgary. Baptism wasn't treated as the finish line.
From that day the focus shifted to helping her keep growing through
one-on-one discipleship, with next steps that include biblical
counseling for healing from childhood trauma, forgiveness, and freedom
in Christ.
