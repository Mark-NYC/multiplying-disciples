Two veterans crossed paths while exercising, and a lighthearted exchange
turned into a real conversation about life and faith. Walking through a
Story of Hope with a Discovery approach, the other veteran shared honest
insights about God and about himself. He admitted he wanted to share his
faith but often worried about offending people when he talked about
Jesus. After hearing a simple 15-second testimony and learning how to
transition naturally into a Story of Hope, he shared his own testimony —
and left with the confidence to start sharing it with others.
