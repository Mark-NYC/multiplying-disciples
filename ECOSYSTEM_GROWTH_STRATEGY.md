# Ecosystem Growth Strategy

## Core Operating Line

**Rank broad. Answer fast. Route wisely. Train the obedient. Track fruit.**

This file defines how MultiplyingDisciples.us fits into the broader ecosystem and how it should become a top marketing asset without becoming a messy content pile.

This is not only an SEO strategy. It is a disciple-making funnel strategy.

---

## Ecosystem Roles

### MultiplyingDisciples.us

**Role:** Search capture and practical disciple-making library.

This site attracts people searching for biblical, practical, and movement-related answers.

Primary job:

- capture broad and practical search demand
- answer questions clearly
- move readers toward obedience
- route the right people into tools, training, and community
- build topical authority around disciple making

Multiplying Disciples should not feel like a ministry brochure.

It should feel like:

> Simple biblical tools for ordinary disciples who want to obey Jesus and multiply.

---

### Obey.Tools

**Role:** Practice layer.

Obey.Tools is where interested readers become obedient practitioners.

Primary job:

- give people simple tools to obey immediately
- help people track relationships, conversations, testimony, gospel sharing, discovery, and church formation
- reveal who is actually willing to act

Key tools:

- Conversation Box
- 4 Questions
- Stories of Hope
- Commands of Christ
- Church Assessment
- 3 Circles
- Prayer tools where appropriate

---

### CoVo Multipliers

**Role:** Training, tribe, and marketplace pathway.

CoVo Multipliers is for people who want to make disciples through ordinary life and work, especially marketplace multipliers, tentmakers, and sentrepreneurs.

Primary job:

- train faithful practitioners
- gather people into shared practice
- connect vocation and disciple making
- develop local stewards
- identify emerging founders
- strengthen city ecosystems

---

### WhatsApp / Field Room

**Role:** Warm community and ongoing activation.

Primary job:

- keep people practicing
- share prompts and stories
- invite simple obedience
- create low-friction next steps
- surface faithful people for training

---

### Immersions / Cohorts / Collaboration

**Role:** Deeper commitment and deployment.

Primary job:

- train practitioners hands-on
- identify faithful multipliers
- connect city-to-city collaboration
- support emerging hubs

---

### Hubs

**Role:** Fruit tracking and long-term multiplication.

Primary job:

- track disciples, leaders, churches, and businesses
- identify local stewards
- strengthen multi-generational fruit
- send multipliers to new peoples and places

---

## Ecosystem Summary

Use this language consistently:

> Multiplying Disciples attracts the curious.

## Site Strategy

MultiplyingDisciples.us should become a structured disciple-making library.

It should not be a random blog.

Every article should do five things:

1. Answer the search intent fast.
2. Give biblical clarity.
3. Provide a practical next step.
4. Route the reader to the right tool or pathway.
5. Track the movement from content to obedience.

## Article Creation Rules

No new article should be created until one of these is true:

1. Ubersuggest or another tool validates meaningful search demand.
2. GSC shows impressions for a related query.
3. The article is needed strategically as a hub/support page.
4. The article is needed to route existing traffic better.
5. The article answers a known question from real users.

Every article brief should include:

- target keyword
- search intent
- current SERP notes
- competing pages
- existing related pages
- page type
- CTA level
- target tool/pathway
- internal links to add
- success metric

## Keyword Research Workflow

For each cluster:

1. Export current GSC queries for related pages.
2. Pull Ubersuggest keyword ideas.
3. Record:
   - keyword
   - search volume
   - SEO difficulty
   - CPC if useful
   - intent
   - current ranking page if any
   - competitor URLs
   - content type needed
4. Classify:
   - refresh existing page
   - write new article
   - create hub
   - ignore
5. Add to content backlog.
6. Prioritize by:
   - relevance to obedience
   - ranking opportunity
   - traffic potential
   - CTA fit
   - ecosystem value

Do not create articles only because volume is high.

A lower-volume keyword that produces practitioners is more valuable than broad traffic that never obeys.

## Strategic Principle

Traffic is not the win.

The win is moving the right people from curiosity to obedience.

So the question for every page is:

> What obedient step should this reader take next?

If the page cannot answer that, it is not finished.
