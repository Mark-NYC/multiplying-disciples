# CONTENT_CLUSTER_ROADMAP.md

The long-term content architecture for multiplyingdisciples.us: eight
tightly connected clusters, ~75 cornerstone articles (38 existing
pages reused, ~37 new), organized around one journey:

**Meet Jesus → Share the Gospel → Make Disciples → Train Disciple
Makers → Practice Church → Multiply Leaders → Multiply Churches**

Every cluster has one pillar, a hub page, supporting articles, one
primary CTA, and an explicit bridge to the next step in the journey.
Evidence base: GSC data in URL_INVENTORY.md, the visitor
types/funnel paths in ECOSYSTEM_GROWTH_STRATEGY.md, and the audit in
CONTENT_AUDIT.md. Search-volume claims are limited to what GSC
actually shows; everything else is flagged `hypothesis` per the
strategy doc's own rule.

Legend: ★ = exists today · ☆ = new article to create · (I) = exists
but marked Improve in CONTENT_AUDIT.md.

## Cluster 0 — Meet Jesus (Jesus & the Twelve)

- **Purpose:** capture broad Bible curiosity (the site's largest
  traffic source) and hand curious readers a first step toward Jesus
  and toward the testimony/gospel clusters.
- **Audience:** Bible Learners (visitor type 1) — often not yet
  disciple-makers, sometimes not yet believers.
- **Primary intent:** informational ("12 disciples in order",
  "apostle meaning").
- **Hub:** `/jesus-and-the-twelve/`.
- **Pillar:** ★(I) The 12 Disciples of Jesus, in Order — 116k
  impressions; the front door of the whole site.
- **Supporting:** ★(I) What Does "Apostle" Actually Mean? · ★(I)
  Jesus the Leader (Mark) [shared with Training] · ☆ Who Is Jesus? A
  Plain Introduction (`hypothesis`) · ☆ What Did Jesus Command His
  Followers? (Commands of Christ bridge) · ☆ How Jesus Made Disciples
  (bridge into Making Disciples).
- **Linking:** pillar links out to every disciple's related practice
  (testimony, gospel) plus the two bridges; bridges link forward to
  Cluster 1/3 pillars.
- **Primary CTA:** Tool — 7 Stories of Hope (for the spiritually
  curious); Commands of Christ (obey.tools) on the commands article.
- **Priority:** pillar CTR work is Phase 1; new articles Phase 3.
- **Business value:** High indirectly — it feeds every other cluster;
  low direct conversion.
- **Size:** 6 articles (3★ / 3☆).

## Cluster 1 — Christian Testimony

- **Purpose:** own practical testimony search end-to-end; the
  easiest first obedience step for an ordinary Christian.
- **Audience:** Christians who have never shared their faith out loud.
- **Primary intent:** "how do I write/share my testimony" +
  "testimony examples" (GSC-validated query family — see
  ECOSYSTEM_GROWTH_STRATEGY.md Cluster 1).
- **Hub:** `/testimony/`.
- **Pillar:** ☆ **How to Share Your Christian Testimony** — the head
  term the cluster currently lacks; existing pages rank for fragments
  of it.
- **Supporting:** ★ Simple 15-Second Testimony Examples · ★ Testimony
  in the Bible: 20 Verses (also covers "Bible verses about testimony"
  — no separate verses article) · ★ 7 Biblical Reasons You Can't Stay
  Silent · ☆ Short Christian Testimony Examples (GSC-validated) · ☆
  How to Write Your Testimony (with the before/exchange/after
  structure) · ☆ Three-Minute Testimony · ☆ Sharing Your Testimony at
  Work or in Church (`hypothesis`) · ☆ Testimony vs. Gospel: What's
  the Difference (bridge to Cluster 2).
- **Linking:** every supporting article links up to the pillar and
  sideways to the 15-Second tool; pillar links forward to How to
  Share the Gospel.
- **Primary CTA:** **Practice** (tool: the 15-Second Testimony
  exercise); Lab as the secondary on the pillar.
- **Priority:** Phase 1 (pillar + 2 new) — the cluster already ranks;
  completing it is cheap and compounding.
- **Business value:** Very high — proven impressions, lowest-friction
  practice step, direct lab feeder.
- **Size:** 9 articles (4★ / 5☆).

## Clusters evaluated and NOT recommended (for now)

- **Discovery Bible Study / Bible Studies** — real demand family, but
  7 Stories of Hope + Three Thirds already occupy the site's lane;
  a DBS-branded cluster would compete with better-established sites
  head-on. Revisit after Phase 2 with GSC evidence.
- **Baptism / Obedience as standalone clusters** — one article each
  inside Simple Church / Making Disciples is the right weight; a full
  cluster would drift from the practical lane into general doctrine.
- **Missionary Vision / Everyday Mission** — covered by the About
  pages and the Multiplier Mandate; no distinct query family in GSC.
- **Kingdom Ministry / Commands of Christ as clusters** — they are
  **destinations**, not clusters: every cluster routes to them via
  CTAs and bridge articles rather than growing rival article sets
  (Commands of Christ content lives on obey.tools by design).
- **Strategy Coordinator** — excluded by direction.

## Implementation phases

### Phase 1 — ~15 items, highest ROI
Ordered by (proven impressions × conversion proximity):

1. **12 Disciples CTR + internal-link pass** (116k impressions at
   0.28% CTR — every 0.1% CTR gained ≈ +40 visits/quarter, and the
   page currently sends readers almost nowhere).
2. **Bible Verses About Spreading the Gospel CTR pass** (8.3k
   impressions, 2 clicks).
3. **How to Evangelize CTR pass** (1.8k impressions, 0 clicks).
4. **Apostles Meaning CTR pass** (2.4k impressions, 0 clicks).
5. ☆ How to Share Your Christian Testimony (pillar — cluster already
   ranks around the hole).
6. ☆ Short Christian Testimony Examples (GSC-validated queries).
7. ☆ How to Write Your Testimony.
8. ☆ How to Make Disciples (pillar).
9. ☆ What Is a Disciple?
10. ☆ How to Share the Gospel (pillar).
11. ☆ What Is the Gospel?
12. ☆ Disciple Training pillar + **new `/disciple-training/` hub**.
13. **Kingdom Ministry Training page upgrade** (business priority;
    every Training CTA lands here).
14. **Person of Peace expansion** (head term, existing thin page).
15. ☆ What Are the Commands of Christ? (bridge; the menu now points
    at obey.tools — this article gives search a reason to arrive).

Why this order: items 1–4 are pure CTR arbitrage on impressions the
site already earns (fastest possible wins, no new content); 5–11
complete the three journey-core clusters where partial rankings prove
authority; 12–13 build the highest-converting cluster; 14–15 are
head-term/bridge singles with outsized strategic value.

### Phase 2 — ~25 articles
Finish Testimony (3-minute, at-work, testimony-vs-gospel), the Gospel
situational set (work, family, questions, introverts, 4 Responses
expansion), Making Disciples support (one-on-one, follow-up,
obedience-based, questions), Training support (curriculum, Jesus
trained the Twelve, evangelism training, new believers), Simple
Church core (How to Start, House vs Simple, What Makes a Church,
First Gathering), Oikos + Pray for Lost Friends, and the CPM pillar.
Rationale: completes every cluster's minimum viable shape so internal
linking and topical authority compound before long-tail expansion.

### Phase 3 — remainder
Leadership new articles (MAWL, Generational Mapping, Raising
Leaders), Meet Jesus additions (Who Is Jesus, What Did Jesus Command,
How Jesus Made Disciples), Simple Church long-tail (Baptism, Luke
10), the Muddy Boots merge + redirect, hub intro upgrades, and the
consolidation/refresh backlog from CONTENT_AUDIT.md. Rationale:
valuable but dependent on the spine existing first.
