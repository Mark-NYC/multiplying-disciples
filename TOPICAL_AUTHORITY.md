# TOPICAL_AUTHORITY.md

Cluster-by-cluster topical-authority ranking from the 2026-07 audit,
**strongest to weakest**. Inputs: inbound-link counts from the live
content graph (see INTERNAL_LINKING.md), GSC evidence recorded in
URL_INVENTORY.md / CONTENT_AUDIT.md, cluster completeness against
CONTENT_CLUSTER_ROADMAP.md, and CTA alignment against CTA_GUIDE.md.

"Linking health" = do pillar↔support links exist both ways, are there
orphans, do readers have an obvious next step.

---

## 1. Sharing the Gospel — strongest

**Why:** The only cluster with everything: proven rankings (3 Circles
62 clicks / 6.7k impressions — best CTR on the site; 7 Stories 75
clicks — most clicks; verses page 8.3k impressions), three hub pages
(`/share-the-gospel/`, `/3-circles-guide/`, `/stories-of-hope/`), the
most-linked editorial article on the site (3 Circles guide, 20
inbound), and a clean article↔tool pairing (guide ↔ slides ↔
sticker). Lab CTAs are consistent.

**Missing content:** the head-term pillar (How to Share the Gospel)
and the definitional anchor (What Is the Gospel?); situational
long-tail (work / family / introverts / 20 questions).

**Linking health: good.** Pillar-fragment pages interlink
reciprocally; person-of-peace links up; this audit added the
verses-page → testimony link. Weak spot: 4 Responses is a 35-word
stub sharing a slide deck with the Church Waffle page (merge or
expand — see INTERNAL_LINKING.md).

**Recommendations:** write the two missing head pages first (roadmap
Phase 1 items 10–11); keep How-to-Evangelize and the future pillar on
separate query intents; resolve the 4 Responses duplication.

## 2. Testimony

**Why:** ~17.5k combined impressions across three articles that form
a genuinely reciprocal triangle (every article links the other two in
body and box). Lowest-friction practice step on the site, direct lab
feeder, and now bridged forward to the gospel cluster (7 Biblical
Reasons → 3 Circles, added this audit).

**Missing content:** the pillar (How to Share Your Christian
Testimony) plus How to Write / Short Examples / 3-Minute — the
cluster ranks on fragments of a head term nothing targets.

**Linking health: excellent** — the model cluster. No orphans, tight
interior triangle, hub `next_step` points at the practice tool.

**Recommendations:** this is the cheapest cluster to complete
(roadmap Phase 1 items 5–7). When the pillar lands, it takes the #1
slot in the hub's `key_articles` and every triangle article adds it
to `related_articles`.

## 3. Simple Church & Practice Tools

**Why:** The site's practice backbone: Three Thirds (16 inbound) and
Church Waffle (13 inbound) are the two most-linked practice pages,
each with real GSC traction (T1). What Is a Simple Church is a true
definitional pillar in the making. Differentiator content — few
competitors answer these practically.

**Missing content:** the how-to spine — How to Start a Simple Church,
What Makes a Church a Church, First Gathering, Baptism, Commands of
Christ bridge. The pillar itself needs expansion (857 words, 0 clicks
on 142 impressions).

**Linking health: good.** Tools are heavily cited from other
clusters (this audit added 10-Qualities → Simple Church and Power of
Multiplication → Simple Church upward links). 4 Responses stub is the
one soft spot.

**Recommendations:** expand the pillar (CTR + depth pass), then the
how-to twin. The cluster's authority currently rests on tools; the
article layer is what search can rank.

## 4. Leadership & Multiplication (CPM)

**Why #4 not higher:** the deepest library (13+ articles) and the
richest interior after this audit's fixes — but no pillar owns the
head term, and per-page traffic is thin (mostly T3). Authority here
is breadth without a center.

**Missing content:** What Is a Church Planting Movement? (the single
highest-leverage missing page for this cluster) · MAWL · Generational
Mapping · How to Raise Up Leaders.

**Linking health: much improved this audit.** Fixed: Lone Wolf +
Trustworthy Leaders were hub-less orphans (now in the CPM hub with
reciprocal links); Multiplier Mandate, Paul in Ephesus, and Pastors'
Objections each had ≤1 inbound link (now 2–3 with visible related
slots and in-body links); the leadership-character spine
(Trustworthy ↔ Lone Wolf ↔ 5 Ways ↔ Jesus the Leader) now exists.
Muddy Boots remains a thin duplicate awaiting its merge.

**Recommendations:** write the CPM pillar and roll every movement
article up to it; switch this cluster's default CTAs from Lab to
Community per the roadmap's CTA table (practitioners need peers, not
intro labs); execute the Muddy Boots merge.

## 5. Meet Jesus (Jesus & the Twelve)

**Why:** One colossal asset — 12 Disciples at 116k impressions, ~45%
of the site's total — now linking out to practice articles (8 body
links) with a filled 3-slot related box (Jesus the Leader added this
audit). But it's a 4-article cluster; the authority is broad traffic,
not topical depth, and CTR (0.28%) wastes most of it.

**Missing content:** Who Is Jesus? · What Did Jesus Command His
Followers? · How Jesus Made Disciples — the bridges that turn Bible
curiosity into the journey's first step.

**Linking health: fair.** Interior links are reciprocal and the hub
is clean; the weakness is *conversion* of its traffic, not its graph.
CTA is `community` on all four articles — the roadmap argues Tool
(7 Stories) fits spiritually-curious readers better.

**Recommendations:** the CTR/title pass on 12 Disciples and Apostles
Meaning outranks any new content sitewide (roadmap Phase 1 items 1,
4); then the three bridge articles.

## 6. Making Disciples

**Why #6 despite being the namesake:** Understanding Biblical
Discipleship is a strong de facto pillar (13 inbound, carries four
legacy redirects) and 3 Core Habits / Friendly Accountability are
solid — but the cluster misses both head terms ("how to make
disciples", "what is a disciple") that the brand is literally named
for, and redirect-equity proves the site used to rank for them.

**Missing content:** How to Make Disciples (pillar) · What Is a
Disciple? · One-on-One Discipleship · First 30 Days · Obedience-Based
Discipleship.

**Linking health: good.** Friendly Accountability's box was
repointed this audit from generic tool pages to its real siblings
(Lone Wolf, 3 Core Habits). Clear Pathway (shared with Four Fields)
gained five in-body framework links.

**Recommendations:** the two head-term pages are roadmap Phase 1
items 8–9; until they exist, keep routing definitional mentions to
Understanding Biblical Discipleship.

## 7. Prayer & the Field

**Why:** Three genuinely good articles with real (modest) GSC
traction and a perfectly reciprocal interior; prayer-walking is a
legitimate pillar. Small but healthy — ranked low on size, not
quality.

**Missing content:** the field half — What Is an Oikos? · How to Pray
for Lost Friends · Luke 10 sending pattern; and Person of Peace lives
in the gospel hub, so the planned oikos ↔ person-of-peace ↔
lost-friends triangle has one existing corner and it's in another
cluster.

**Linking health: good and improved** — hub `next_step` added this
audit; prayer-walking and 10-Qualities now link into 12 Key Prayer
Points; the cluster links outward but receives little from outside
(only the CPM prayer link so far).

**Recommendations:** write the oikos explainer first (the tool
already appears inside 3 Core Habits with no page owning the term);
decide Person of Peace's hub when the triangle exists — don't move it
before there's something to move it into.

## 8. Disciple Training — weakest, highest stakes

**Why weakest:** the only major cluster with **no hub, no pillar, and
no article targeting its query family** ("discipleship training",
"training curriculum", "evangelism training") — while being the
shortest path from search to the ministry's actual offering. Kingdom
Ministry Training, the named business priority, is a slide-link
landing page that until this audit had zero contextual inbound links
and an empty related box.

**Missing content:** everything structural — `/disciple-training/`
hub · Disciple Training pillar · curriculum article · Commands of
Christ training bridge · KMT page upgrade to a real training landing.

**Linking health: poor but no longer orphaned.** This audit gave KMT
a related box (Free Training · Content vs. Systems · Four Fields
training) and a contextual inbound link from Content vs. Systems; it
was already in the hamburger menu. Four Fields training (495
impressions with zero optimization) proves latent demand.

**Recommendations:** roadmap Phase 1 items 12–13 (hub + pillar + KMT
upgrade) are the highest-conversion work available. Once the hub
exists, reassign `hub:` on Four Fields training, Content vs. Systems,
and Jesus the Leader per the roadmap's split, and point every
training-cluster CTA at KMT.
