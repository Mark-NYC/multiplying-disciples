# CONTENT_GAP_ANALYSIS.md

Where the current library falls short of the cluster architecture in
CONTENT_CLUSTER_ROADMAP.md, with the evidence for each gap. Rule
inherited from ECOSYSTEM_GROWTH_STRATEGY.md: no invented search
volume — every gap is labeled with its evidence class:

- **GSC** — the site already earns impressions/clicks in this family.
- **redirect-equity** — a redirected legacy URL proves past rankings.
- **structural** — the cluster logically requires it (pillar/bridge).
- **hypothesis** — brand-fit guess; validate volume before writing.

## 1. Missing pillars (the biggest structural gap)

The site has strong *supporting* articles but almost no *head-term*
pages. Five of eight clusters lack a pillar:

| Missing pillar | Cluster | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| How to Share Your Christian Testimony | Testimony | GSC — cluster pages earn ~17.5k combined impressions on fragment queries; nothing targets the head "how to share your testimony". |
| How to Share the Gospel | Sharing the Gospel | GSC — verses page alone has 8.3k impressions; "how to evangelize" page exists but the larger "share the gospel" family is unowned. |
| How to Make Disciples | Making Disciples | redirect-equity — "ridiculously-simple-3-ways-to-make-disciples" and three sibling URLs ranked and now redirect to a page not built for that query. |
| Disciple Training (pillar + hub) | Disciple Training | GSC (four-fields-training: 495 impressions with zero optimization) + business priority; the whole cluster routes to KMT/labs. |
| What Is a Church Planting Movement? | Leadership | structural — 8+ existing CPM articles orbit a missing definitional center; DMM has one, CPM does not. |

## 2. Missing definitional cornerstones

"What is X" pages that every cluster needs for internal-link anchors
and featured-snippet capture:

- What Is the Gospel? (structural — every gospel article assumes it)
- What Is a Disciple? (structural; redirect-equity via
  "discovering-the-disciple-meaning")
- What Is an Oikos? (structural; the tool exists in article bodies —
  3 Core Habits teaches oikos mapping — but no page owns the term)
- What Are the Commands of Christ? (structural + business — the menu
  and CTAs now point to obey.tools with no on-site page explaining
  the thing being linked to)

## 3. Situational long-tail (highest practical usefulness)

The library explains methods but rarely situations. Ordinary
Christians search situations:

- Sharing the gospel at work / with family (hypothesis)
- Sharing your testimony at work or in church (hypothesis; the
  strategy doc lists "testimony in church example" as a known GSC
  query theme)
- Evangelism for introverts (hypothesis)
- New believer follow-up / first 30 days (structural — the site
  teaches 4-1-1 and follow-up tools with no article entry point)

## 4. The training gap (biggest commercial gap)

Kingdom Ministry Training — a named business priority with live
training content — is a 430-word slide-link page with 19 impressions.
Nothing on the site targets "discipleship training", "training
curriculum", or "evangelism training". Meanwhile the redirected
legacy URL "equipping-your-church-for-disciple-making-movements-a-
pastors-guide-to-effective-training" (71 impressions, tier-3) proves
the family ranked before. This is the largest mismatch between what
the ministry sells and what the site says.

## 5. The practice-church gap

Simple Church has definitions (What Is a Simple Church) and tools
(Church Waffle, Three Thirds) but no how-to spine: How to Start a
Simple Church, Your First Gathering, House Church vs Simple Church,
What Makes a Church a Church, Who Can Baptize. Competitor content in
this family is largely theoretical — practical, checklist-grade pages
are the differentiation opportunity (hypothesis on volume, high
confidence on fit).

## 6. CTR gaps on existing rankings (not content gaps, but bigger)

Four pages earn ~129k combined impressions with near-zero CTR:
12 Disciples (0.28%), Spreading-the-Gospel verses (0.02%), How to
Evangelize (0%), Apostles Meaning (0%). Fixing titles/metas/snippet
intent on these four out-earns any ten new articles this quarter.
(GSC; Phase 1 items 1–4.)

## 7. Orphan and stub debt

- Hub-less articles: Lone Wolf, Trustworthy Leaders,
  Strategy Coordinator (deliberate), 4-1-1 — the first two need hub
  assignment so cluster linking can reach them.
- Stubs behind named tools: 4 Responses to the Gospel (37 words),
  4-1-1 (35 words) — tool pages are fine as stubs, but 4 Responses is
  positioned as an article and should be expanded (audit: Improve).
- One thin duplicate: Muddy Boots vs Clear Pathway (merge, audit).

## 8. Topics deliberately left ungapped

Discovery Bible Study (occupied lane — 7 Stories is the site's
answer), general apologetics, worship/liturgy, denominational
debates, devotional content, book reviews (legacy reviews were
redirected on purpose). These are off-lane for "practical
disciple-making" and would dilute topical authority.
